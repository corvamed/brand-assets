# Corvamed brand assets

<img src="logo.svg" alt="Corvamed logo" width="360">

Official logos, colours and images of [Corvamed](https://corvamed.com), an AI service in development that helps patients find clinical trials they may qualify for and helps research teams reach the right participants.

## Typography

- **Jost** (weight 500) for headings.
- **Commissioner** (weights 400, 500 and 600) for body text.

Both fonts are published under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org). They are not included in this repository; the website repository includes the web font files with their own licence.

## How to write the name

Write **Corvamed**: one word, capital C, the rest in lower case. Not "CorvaMed", "CORVAMED" or "Corva Med". The domain is written in lower case: corvamed.com.

## Please do not change the logo

Use the files as they are. Do not recolour, stretch, rotate, add effects to or redraw the logo, and do not change the order of the colours in the mark.

If you need a format that is not here, email [info@corvamed.com](mailto:info@corvamed.com).
